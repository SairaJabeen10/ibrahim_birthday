A letter

Happy Birthday, Ibrahim! 🎉❤️

Dear Ibrahim,

Happy Birthday!

Aaj ka din sirf aap ka hai. Main dil se dua karti hoon ke Allah Ta'ala aap ki zindagi ko hamesha khushiyon, barkaton, kamyabiyon aur sukoon se bhar de. Har naya saal aap ke liye naye mauke, khoobsurat yaadein aur beshumar rehmatain lekar aaye.

Aap ki muskurahat aur aap ka achha ikhlaq un khoobsurat baaton mein se hain jo logon ke dil jeet leti hain. Dua hai ke Allah Ta'ala aap ko har qadam par kamyabi ata farmaye, aap ke har jaiz khwab ko poora kare aur aap ko hamesha apni hifazat mein rakhe.

Shukriya har us khoobsurat yaad ke liye jo aap ki wajah se aur bhi khaas bani. Allah kare aap ki har dua qubool ho, har mushkil aasaan ho aur aap ki zindagi hamesha khushiyon aur sukoon se roshan rahe.

Ek baar phir,

Happy Birthday!🥳

Allah Ta'ala aap ko sehat, lambi umr, izzat, kamyabi aur bepanah khushiyan ata farmaye. Ameen!

Best wishes and heartfelt prayers,

With respect and sincerity.

Here's to another year of us

Always yours

Happy Birthday
